# Better Auth Flutterwave Domain Context

## Billing reference

The local owner of billing state. It is either a Better Auth user ID or an organization ID whose
member is authorized to perform billing actions. It is not a Flutterwave customer identifier.

## Transaction

A locally persisted checkout or charge. `txRef` is generated by this plugin; `transactionId` and
`flwRef` are Flutterwave identifiers. Fulfillment requires a verified successful status and exact
`txRef`, amount, and currency match.

## Native subscription

A Flutterwave subscription produced by a successful card checkout carrying a numeric
`paymentPlanId`. The plugin resolves it using the verified transaction, billing email, and plan.
There is no provider subscription-create call.

## Local subscription

A subscription whose renewals, groups, seats, and limits are stored by the plugin. Automated trial
transitions, scheduled plan changes, and proration are deferred. Reusable charge tokens are encrypted at rest; absent
a token, renewal falls back to hosted checkout and remains pending until verification.

## Product and plan

Products and inventory are local catalog records. Flutterwave payment plans may be synchronized,
but the plugin does not claim that Flutterwave has a Paystack-like remote product catalog.

## Organization billing

Local ownership and authorization for organization subscriptions. Flutterwave billing identity is
the immutable email stored on the subscription; no customer record is added to the Better Auth user
or organization.

## Marketplace split

An authorized checkout allocation to existing KYC-approved Flutterwave subaccounts. Allowed
subaccount IDs and default split rules are configured separately from organization billing.

## Webhook event

An idempotently persisted provider delivery. Authenticity is HMAC-SHA256 over the exact raw body
using `flutterwave-signature` and the configured `secretHash`. Payment events are re-verified before
they change entitlements.

## Flutterwave adapter

The typed, Zod-validated boundary around the untyped CommonJS `flutterwave-node-v3` SDK and the
narrow direct HTTP client used for unsupported operations such as Flutterwave Standard checkout.
Only Promise APIs are used.
